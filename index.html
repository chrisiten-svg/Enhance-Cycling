Rules and setup
  1. Off-season: 2 sessions a week, alternating Day A and Day B. Race season: 1 Season session a week.
  2. Weeks 1 to 4 use 3 x 8 to 10 at 2 to 3 reps in the tank. After that, go heavy at 4 to 6 reps.
  3. Up fast, down controlled. When every set hits the top of the rep range, add load next time.
  4. Ride first, lift at least 6 hours later. No heavy legs within 48 hours of a race or key interval day.
  5. Short on time? Do the first two lifts and leave.
  6. Knee care: run the Knee tab as your warm-up before every lifting day, plus once more each week.
  7. Most cycling knee pain comes from bike fit and sudden jumps in riding volume. Check saddle height and cleat position, and build hours gradually.
  8. Knee pain that lasts past the next morning, or any swelling, means back off and get it checked.
  9. Machines are fine. Free weights carry over a bit better for balance, but a machine you use every week beats a barbell you skip.
  10. Install on Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen.
  11. Your checks, loads and log are saved in this phone's browser only. Clearing browser data erases them.
